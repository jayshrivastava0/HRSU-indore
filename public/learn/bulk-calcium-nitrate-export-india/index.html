How to buy bulk calcium nitrate from India: pricing, MOQ, documents and shipping

Last updated: 2 July 2026  ·  Reviewed by Sujay Shrivastava - Digital Transformation Consultant
In summary

HRSU Indore is a manufacturer that exports 100% water-soluble calcium nitrate powder to 30+ countries via Mundra Port. Orders run from pallets to full containers, packed in 25 kg or 50 kg HDPE bags. We prepare export documents in-house (invoice, packing list, B/L, COO, COA, TDS/SDS) and accept T/T, LC and advance payment. Send your volume and destination for a firm quote.

What drives the price

Bulk calcium nitrate pricing is quoted per metric tonne and moves with a few factors:

  • Grade and purity — fully soluble powder vs lower grades.
  • Packaging — 25 kg vs 50 kg bags, palletisation, liners.
  • Incoterms — Ex-Works, FOB Mundra, or CIF destination.
  • Order volume — part-container vs full-container loads.
  • Freight and FX — ocean freight and currency at time of order.

For domestic reference, HRSU's retail store lists 25 kg at ₹750 and 50 kg at ₹1,500 (MRP incl. taxes). Bulk export FOB pricing from major Indian ports (Mundra / Nhava Sheva) currently ranges from $300 to $420 USD per Metric Ton, contingent on the specific grade (100% water-soluble agricultural crystalline vs 98% industrial), inclusion of additives (boron, etc.), and total order volume. Final pricing is provided on request after reviewing your exact specifications and volume.

Production lead time and maritime transit

Accepted payment terms: T/T (telegraphic transfer), LC (letter of credit), and advance payment, agreed per order and buyer relationship.

Factory to port: Standard dispatch, inland transit, and customs clearance take 3 to 7 days following order confirmation and financial instruments (such as Letter of Credit). This efficiency reflects direct proximity to Mundra Port.

Ocean transit from Mundra Port: Varies by destination. Middle East (4–10 days), Southeast Asia (10–22 days), East Asia (18–25 days), Europe (25–35 days), North America West Coast (28–35 days), and North America East Coast (35–45+ days). Transit times depend on vessel scheduling, containerization density, transshipment hub routing, and geopolitical conditions affecting maritime chokepoints (such as the Suez Canal).

What is the price of calcium nitrate in India?

Last updated: 4 July 2026  ·  Reviewed by Sujay Shrivastava - Digital Transformation Consultant
In summary

HRSU Indore's published retail price for fertilizer-grade calcium nitrate is ₹30 per kg — sold as ₹750 for a 25 kg bag or ₹1,500 for a 50 kg bag, MRP inclusive of all taxes. Bulk and container/export pricing is quoted separately and depends on quantity, grade, packaging and Incoterms.

Both sizes are 100% water-soluble, 18.5–19% Ca, 15.5%+ nitrate-N, with a Certificate of Analysis on every batch. Prices are MRP, inclusive of all taxes under the Indian Legal Metrology Act.

What drives price differences between suppliers

  • Grade/purity — percent Ca and N, and insoluble residue level (lower-purity products are sometimes priced lower but leave residue that clogs fertigation equipment).
  • Packaging — bag quality (HDPE), weight accuracy, and moisture protection.
  • Quantity — retail bag price vs bulk/container pricing differ substantially; larger orders typically get a better per-kg rate.
  • Documentation — whether a Certificate of Analysis, TDS and SDS are included.

Bulk & export (container-load) pricing

For 1 MT+ container orders shipped via Mundra Port, pricing is quoted on a FOB/CIF basis and depends on order quantity, packaging, destination country and current freight/currency rates. ⚠ Export pricing is not fixed — request a current quote for your specific volume and Incoterm.

About

Who is behind the product.

A small, founder-led manufacturer of industrial-grade calcium nitrate, with the registrations and the plant address in plain view.

Who we are

A manufacturer, not a trading desk.

HRSU Indore Pvt. Ltd. makes calcium nitrate tetrahydrate at its own plant in Maksi, Madhya Pradesh, India. The company was incorporated on 25 July 2022 and is a member of the Federation of Indian Export Organisations (FIEO).

The founder has worked in the chemical industry for more than 20 years, and an affiliated trading firm has been in business for more than 15 years. The plant, the product and the paperwork are in one company.

We are new to export. We have not yet shipped an export order, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise. What we can show you is the product specification, a real certificate of analysis, the plant address and the company registrations below, so you can check us before you place an order. Our first-order process is designed around that: you decide how much risk to take.

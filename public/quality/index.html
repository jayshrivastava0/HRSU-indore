Checking us

How you can verify the result.

1

Ask for a sample

Samples are available. Test them in your own laboratory against the COA before you commit to a container.

2

Independent inspection

You are welcome to appoint an independent inspection company, for example SGS, Bureau Veritas or Intertek, to sample and inspect before shipment, at your cost. We will give them access.

3

Compare to the COA

Every lot ships with its COA. If a result on your own test differs, send us both reports and we will review them together.

4

Pay by letter of credit

Payment is by T/T or LC. An LC lets your bank release payment only against the documents you specified.

Next step

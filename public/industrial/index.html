Common questions

Quick answers.

What grade is this?

Industrial grade calcium nitrate tetrahydrate, Ca(NO₃)₂·4H₂O, with an assay of 99–103%. We supply industrial buyers.

Is the roughly 30% water a quality problem?

No. About 30.5% of the crystal is water of crystallisation, built into the hydrate (calculated from the formula). It is not free moisture.

What is the minimum order?

Container scale. The TDS lists 10 MT. If you want a smaller trial quantity, ask us in the form.

Which documents do you provide?

TDS, SDS and a COA for each lot, plus export documents with the shipment.

Do you sell small bags?

Not on this page. Small retail packs for farms and growers in India are sold separately in our retail store.

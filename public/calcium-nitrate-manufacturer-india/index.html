What HRSU Indore makes

One product, made and documented for industrial buyers: calcium nitrate tetrahydrate with an assay of 99–103%. Buyers use it as a chloride-free concrete set accelerator, for hydrogen sulphide and odour control in wastewater, in mining (cold-weather concrete and sulfide control), in oil and gas fluids and in latex coagulation. Full specification, packing and ordering steps are on the industrial grade page.

Buying calcium nitrate in India: manufacturer, trader or directory listing?

Calcium nitrate was long imported into India. Gujarat State Fertilizers & Chemicals announced in October 2020 that it would make the product domestically, with an initial capacity of about 10,000 tonnes a year, describing it as a first for India.[2] Beyond the larger producers, most listings on B2B directories are traders or small sellers, and directory entries are self-reported, so the same company can appear with different addresses and prices. Before you order from any supplier in India, including HRSU Indore:

  • Check the GSTIN on the government GST portal and match the legal name and address.
  • Ask whether the seller manufactures the product, and for the plant address and a video or visit.
  • Ask for a recent batch COA, the TDS and the SDS, and check the grade and assay against what you need.
  • Confirm the packing, HS code and Incoterm, and for export orders the exporter's IEC and the ports used.
  • Compare landed cost, not only the ex-works price; see our India vs China sourcing guide.

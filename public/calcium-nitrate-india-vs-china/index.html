How to compare two quotes properly

  1. Ask both suppliers for the same grade (calcium nitrate tetrahydrate, assay range) and packing (for example 50 kg bags).
  2. Ask for the same Incoterm, ideally CIF or DDP to your port, so freight and insurance are inside the price.
  3. Request the TDS, SDS and a recent batch COA before you decide, and check the assay and impurity limits against your use.
  4. Add duties and local handling at your destination to get landed cost per tonne.
  5. Ask for payment terms (T/T or letter of credit) and the confirmed lead time in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Is calcium nitrate cheaper from China or India?

On the one public price tracker we found, China is cheaper per kilogram. Expert Market Research lists about US$0.284/kg for China and about US$0.398/kg for India in the quarter ending June 2026, and forecasts US$0.28–0.32 and US$0.40–0.45 for the second half of 2026. The tracker does not state the price basis, so treat it as a direction, and compare written quotes on the same Incoterm and grade.

Why would an importer buy calcium nitrate from India instead of China?

Common reasons are a second source to reduce dependence on one country, container-scale lots from about 10 MT, Indian-origin documentation, and a supplier that can quote CIF or DDP to your port. Price per kilogram is not one of the reasons the data supports. Duties on Chinese- and Indian-origin goods differ by destination and change, so check current rates for HS 2834.29.

What minimum order does HRSU Indore require?

The technical data sheet lists a 10 MT minimum, in 50 kg bags with an inner liner. Ask about trial quantities.

What should I compare between two calcium nitrate quotes?

Grade and assay (for example 99–103% as the tetrahydrate), packing, Incoterm and port, payment terms, documents (TDS, SDS, COA, certificate of origin), lead time, and the full landed cost including freight, insurance and duties.

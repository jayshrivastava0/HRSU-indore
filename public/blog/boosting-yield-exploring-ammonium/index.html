Boosting Yield: Exploring Ammonium Nitrate Alternatives

Category: Mining

The mining industry faces increasing pressure to maximize yields while simultaneously navigating tighter regulations and supply chain vulnerabilities. Traditional explosives, heavily reliant on ammonium nitrate, are increasingly scrutinized due to safety concerns and environmental impact. Procurement managers and factory owners need solutions that deliver consistent performance and mitigate risk – solutions that directly impact the bottom line.

The Operational Challenge

Standard chemical explosives often fall short of delivering optimal fragmentation and consistent power, leading to reduced ore recovery and increased operational costs. The inherent instability of ammonium nitrate, coupled with the potential for accidental detonation, presents significant safety hazards and regulatory hurdles. Many manufacturers are actively seeking alternatives to ensure operational safety and regulatory compliance.

Your first order

How a first order works, step by step.

A new supplier is a risk. This is the order of events, what we need from you, and the checks you can build in.

Step by step

From first enquiry to your warehouse.

A new supplier is a risk, so the order is built in stages. You can stop after any of them.

1

Tell us what you need

Application, quantity, destination port and any documents your importer requires. Two lines is enough.

2

We quote

Price, lead time, Incoterm (EXW, FOB, CIF or DDP) and payment terms, with the TDS, SDS and a sample COA, within one business day.

3

Test a sample

Samples are available. Run them against the COA in your own laboratory. Ask us about a smaller trial quantity; the TDS lists 10 MT as the standard minimum.

4

Agree the order

We confirm the specification, packing, documents and payment, by T/T or by letter of credit.

5

Production and packing

Packed in 50 kg PP woven bags with an inner plastic liner, marked with the product, net weight and our plant address.

6

Optional inspection

You may appoint an independent inspector (SGS, Bureau Veritas, Intertek or similar), at your cost, to sample and inspect before shipment.

7

Shipment

From Mundra Port or Nhava Sheva. Calcium nitrate travels as UN1454, Class 5.1, PG III, and we declare it that way.

8

Documents

Commercial invoice, bill of lading, certificate of origin, the COA for each lot and the export paperwork, prepared in-house.

Send us this

What helps us quote quickly.

  • Application (concrete admixture, wastewater, mining, oil and gas, process industry)
  • Quantity, for example one 20 ft container, about 20 MT
  • Destination port and country
  • Preferred Incoterm and payment method
  • Any documents your importer needs, such as an SDS in a particular format or a certificate of origin

Importing into the EU, US or Australia? Tell us the destination and we will go through REACH, TSCA or AICIS requirements with you before you order.

Why Powdered Calcium Nitrate is Superior for Latex Dipping

Category: Latex Rubber

In the competitive world of latex dipping—whether for surgical gloves, industrial balloons, or condoms—consistency is currency. For decades, the industry has relied on standard granular Calcium Nitrate. While functional, granules introduce variables that can quietly kill your yield rates.

1. The Solubility Problem

Standard granules are often coated with anti-caking agents (wax/oil) which create sludge in your tanks. HRSU Powder dissolves instantly with zero residue, keeping your dipping formers spotless.

2. Stable Specific Gravity

If your coagulant takes 20 minutes to dissolve, your tank's specific gravity fluctuates. Our hyper-soluble powder stabilizes instantly, allowing production to start immediately.
